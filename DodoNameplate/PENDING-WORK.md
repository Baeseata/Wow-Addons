- Reading side, for the record: **inside a BG `UnitClassBase` is SECRET** (`/dnp test` on a Temple of Kotmogu enemy printed `class: SECRET`; the contract annotation is `SecretWhenUnitIdentityRestricted`). The one lead worth chasing was `UnitClassFromGUID`, whose contract permits a secret argument and marks only the localized `className` as `ConditionalSecret` — `/dp class` measured **all three returns SECRET** when the GUID is secret, `classFilename` and `classID` included. Secrecy propagates from the argument: `SecretArguments = "AllowedWhenTainted"` means *you may pass one in*, not *you may learn the answer*. There is also no number→colour sink (`C_CurveUtil` only maps booleans) and no per-class boolean for `EvaluateColorValueFromBoolean`, so nothing can paint it. A friendly plate in the same BG returned plaintext and a valid colour, which is the positive control proving the probe worked and the wall is per-unit-identity. Full data + the generalizable lesson: `DodoProbe/CLAUDE.md` §`/dp class`.
